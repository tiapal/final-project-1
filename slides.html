🌿 Virtual Food Expo

A food trade show you can attend from home, in your own language.

Bettina Palang · October 26, 2026

The problem: food trade shows shut people out

  • Importers and distributors find suppliers at shows. Small makers go to find buyers.
  • A 10x10 booth: $4,700 to $4,900 at the Fancy Food Show [1][2]
  • One 4-day show trip: about $4,000 per attendee [3]
  • Anuga 2025: 8,000 exhibitors, 145,000 visitors [4]. Nobody can meet them all.
It started with my dad.
He is a food distributor who used to go to the shows. His anxiety now keeps him home, so he misses the suppliers.

Language is a barrier too.

How the agents work

  • 4 agents on gpt-6-luna
  • Matchmaker: finds vendors
  • Booth assistant: one per vendor, answers only from its data
  • Translator: both ways, checks every number
  • Deal brief: bullet summary
  • Prices and rules are code, not AI

Safety: defenses that were tested

  • Private prices never enter the prompt, plus an output guard
  • 3 injection attacks: 0 leaks
  • Translator checks every number
  • Token caps, 4-step tool limit, rate limits
  • Passwords hashed, lockout, one-time reset links, admin license review
9 of 9 safety checks passed. Safety log shown in the app.

Impact

  • Cost to serve: about half a cent per buyer session, versus a $4,700 booth [1] or a ~$4,000 show trip [3]
  • Measured speed: search 1.5 to 2.5 s, chat reply under 1.1 s (English)
  • Opens trade to people who cannot travel and to buyers and makers who do not share a language
